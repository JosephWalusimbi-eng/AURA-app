AURA privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026. Applies to the AURA app for Android and Windows.

In short

AURA is built to work offline. By default nothing leaves your device and no audio is kept. There is no account, no advertising and no telemetry. The developer cannot see your reminders, conversations or recordings.

What AURA never sends

Your recordings, transcripts, memory, reminders, personal model and voice-setup data are never uploaded by AURA. Speech recognition, training and the optional wake word all run on your device.

The wake word

If you turn on "Listen for AURA" (off by default), the microphone is used continuously on the device. The audio is examined in memory and discarded unless the name "AURA" is heard; it is not stored or sent anywhere. Android shows a notification while it is on.

Network use

AURA needs the network for one thing only: to download a speech model once, from the public Hugging Face repository Walusimbi/whisper-afrispeech-east-africa, when you choose to. The download is checked against a published SHA-256 checksum. Hugging Face receives the usual information any download request carries (such as your IP address). If you point AURA at a local language model on your own machine, that connection is to the address you configure.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to hear your requests, voice setup and the optional wake word.
  • Notifications and alarms: to alert you when a reminder is due.
  • Calendar (optional, Android): only if you switch on "Also add events to my phone calendar".

What is not done yet

Being plain about the limits: the on-device database is not yet encrypted at rest (it relies on your device's own storage protection and screen lock); this is planned. On Android, AURA turns off Google's automatic backup and device-to-device transfer of its data, so recordings and memory are not copied off the phone. On Windows, AURA's data folder is covered by whatever backup tools you use yourself. Reminder text may appear in notifications on a locked screen depending on your device settings.

Children

AURA is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, since it collects nothing off the device.
